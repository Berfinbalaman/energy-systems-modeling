
# Engineering Projects – Energy Systems and Simulation

This repository contains my engineering projects and practical studies focused on energy systems, thermodynamic analysis, and numerical simulation.

As a Mechanical Engineering student at Mersin University, I am interested in developing my technical skills in energy efficiency, thermal systems, and computational engineering.

## 1. MATLAB – Thermodynamic Analysis of a Cogeneration System

This project focuses on the thermodynamic modeling and performance analysis of a cogeneration system using MATLAB.

The model involves thermodynamic calculations and system performance evaluation based on relevant energy balance equations and operating parameters.

The main objective is to understand the thermodynamic behavior of the system and investigate how different parameters affect its performance.

**Skills and concepts:**
- MATLAB programming
- Thermodynamic calculations
- Energy balance analysis
- Cogeneration systems
- Engineering problem-solving

## 2. EnergyPlus – Building Energy Simulation

This project was developed using EnergyPlus, based on practical knowledge gained through an online technical training course.

The original building energy simulation model was modified by adjusting climate conditions and selected numerical parameters.

The purpose of this study is to explore how changes in simulation inputs influence building energy performance, particularly heating and cooling demands.

The project serves as an introductory study in building energy simulation and provides a foundation for further independent analysis.

**Skills and concepts:**
- EnergyPlus
- Building energy modeling
- Heating and cooling demand analysis
- Climate-related simulation parameters
- Building energy performance
